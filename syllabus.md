## Course Description

This course introduces the principles and practical applications of machine learning and artificial intelligence in business analytics. Students will learn how data-driven techniques can support decision-making, improve business processes, and identify patterns in complex datasets.

The course combines conceptual instruction with practical exercises using Python and related analytical tools. Topics include supervised and unsupervised learning, predictive modeling, neural networks, deep learning, text mining, and Generative AI. Students will also explore model evaluation, data preparation, visualization, and the ethical implications of using AI in business settings.

Through assignments, tutorials, presentations, and a group project, students will practice translating business questions into analytical problems, building models, interpreting results, and communicating actionable insights.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students should be able to:

1. Explain the role of machine learning and artificial intelligence in business analytics.
2. Apply supervised and unsupervised learning methods to business problems.
3. Develop and train neural networks for appropriate analytical tasks.
4. Explain and explore applications of Generative AI.
5. Evaluate model performance using appropriate metrics and validation techniques.
6. Use Python libraries and analytical tools to build and assess models.
7. Interpret data and model outputs to develop actionable business insights.
8. Identify ethical, privacy, fairness, and reliability concerns associated with AI.
9. Communicate analytical findings clearly through visualizations, reports, and presentations.

## Course Structure

The course includes lectures, workshops, tutorials, homework assignments, quizzes, presentations, and a group project.

- **Lectures:** Introduce concepts, methods, and applications.

- **Homework:** Reinforces concepts through individual exercises.
- **Quizzes:** Assess understanding of course materials.
- **Selected Topic Presentation/Application:** Allows students to investigate and demonstrate an AI or analytics application.
- **Group Project:** Applies the course concepts to a business or organizational problem.

Students should regularly review course announcements and materials and seek help when concepts or assignments are unclear.

## Group Project

The group project provides an opportunity to apply machine learning and AI techniques to a practical business problem. Students will work collaboratively through problem definition, data collection, preprocessing, model development, evaluation, visualization, and presentation.

### Project Stages

1. **Proposal and Data Collection:** Define the business problem, establish project objectives, identify relevant data, and explain the planned analytical approach.
2. **Model Development:** Prepare the data, select suitable methods, build models, and document the analytical process.
3. **Evaluation and Visualization:** Evaluate model performance, present relevant visualizations, and interpret the findings.
4. **Final Report:** Summarize the problem, data, methodology, results, limitations, and recommendations.
5. **Final Presentation and Demonstration:** Communicate the project's approach, findings, and business implications.

### Project Evaluation

The project is worth 35 points. Evaluation includes participation in scheduled in-class work, meeting attendance, reports, and the final presentation. Peer evaluation acts as a multiplier between zero and one for each applicable group assignment.

Students are expected to contribute meaningfully, communicate with their teammates, document their work, and participate in project meetings. Each member should understand the methods and findings presented by the group.

## Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence

AI tools can support learning, coding, brainstorming, and data analysis, but their use must be responsible and consistent with assignment instructions.

Students should:

- Verify AI-generated explanations, code, and analytical results.
- Protect confidential, personal, and sensitive information.
- Recognize that AI-generated content may contain errors or bias.
- Clearly explain analytical methods and conclusions.
- Follow instructor guidance regarding permitted AI assistance and attribution.

Unless otherwise specified by the instructor, students remain responsible for the accuracy, originality, and integrity of all submitted work.
